credit"`, keep every assertion and gain the invariant prefix:

| New description | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `I12 expiry never claws back credit a hold has reserved` | 500 000 granted, 400 000 held, expiry takes only the unheld 100 000 and leaves `expired_at` unset |

| `I12 a new expiring grant cannot absorb spending that predates it` | spending before the grant existed is not attributed to it |

## The L1 observation

`I12 a release after the grant expired returns spendable credit (L1, fixed in
06a)`. This test asserts what the code does today, names the defect and names
the unit that fixes it. `v1-release.md` 1.1 requires the suite to stay green and
1.2 requirement 6 forbids hiding a skipped test, so a failing test is not an
option; the honest form is a passing test that a later unit inverts.

The script, in micro-dollars:

| Step | Action | balance | held | available | promotional | `expired_at` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | grant 500 000 promotional, `expires_at` 60s in the past | 500 000 | 0 | 500 000 | 500 000 | `nil` |
| 2 | hold 400 000 | 500 000 | 400 000 | 100 000 | 500 000 | `nil` |
| 3 | `Credits.expire_due/0` | 400 000 | 400 000 | 0 | 400 000 | `nil` |
| 4 | release the hold | 400 000 | 0 | **400 000** | 400 000 | `nil` |
| 5 | debit 400 000 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | `nil` |

Step 3 is right and is the invariant working: `expire_locked/3` takes
`min(remaining, promotional, balance - held)`, which is 100 000, and leaves
`expired_at` unset so a later pass can finish the job (`ledger.ex:283-305`).

Step 4 is L1. Expiry is a pass, not a property of the money. The 400 000 the
hold had reserved on a grant that expired an hour ago comes back as ordinary
spendable credit, and step 5 spends it. It stays spendable until some scheduler
happens to run `expire_due/1` again.

**The fix is 06a's.** Its lot model gives each grant its own row, so value
released against an expired lot becomes *expired* rather than spendable, and
06a inverts step 4 and deletes step 5. 01e has already written the pure model's
view of this difference as `I10 lot view: released value on an expired lot
becomes expired, not spendable`, so the model and the database state the same
future behaviour from two directions.

`docs/correctness.md` records L1 under I12's known limits with 06a named.

## What this does not prove

Overlapping promotions in the general case, expiry racing a settlement, and
reserved value on an expired lot are all 06a's, and are listed as `PLANNED
(06a)` in `docs/correctness.md`. The current allocator assumes at most one live
promotional grant per tenant, which is why those three are phase-06 work rather
than a gap here.
