# I05: cluster guarantees match the documented limitations

Build unit 01c. Core at `26e18b6` plus the uncommitted wave 1a and 1b tree.
Elixir 1.20.1, OTP 29, PostgreSQL 16.13 on port 5490, schema version 6.

`AuroraMeter.ClusterConvergenceTest`, four new tests, plus two renamed tests
that stay in `AuroraMeter.ClusterTest`.

## Method, and what it does not cover

Peers are simulated on one VM through `AuroraMeter.Cluster.apply/3`, the
documented test hook, reached by `AuroraMeter.Test.simulate_node/3` (a delta
gossip) and `simulate_flush/3` (a totals announcement). That drives
`Cluster.handle_batch/3`, `Counter.apply_remote/2` and `Counter.rebase/3`
exactly as a received PubSub message would. Origins are literal foreign atoms
(`:n2@test`, `:n3@test`, `:n4@test`) because `handle_batch/3` ignores a batch
whose origin is `node()`.

A peer's *flush* is `Storage.add_counters/1` writing that peer's delta straight
into the database and returning the total, which is what a real node's flush
does, followed by the announcement of that total.

Delay and loss are scripted by withholding or never issuing a call. No
`{:block_until, ref}` rendezvous is used, and the module carries no `:fault`
tag: `Cluster.apply/3` is a synchronous `GenServer.call`, so message order here
*is* call order and there is no race for a rendezvous to arbitrate.
Manufacturing one would be theatre.

**Not covered**: `Phoenix.PubSub` itself, a netsplit, a real rejoin, or a second
BEAM. A multi-node harness belongs to 11d. `docs/correctness.md` states this
under I05 rather than leaving a reader to infer it.

**One downward move is expected and is not a violation.** A node's own flush
re-bases unconditionally on the database total, so a peer's unflushed gossip
leaves the view until that peer flushes. Only a *totals announcement* is
forbidden from lowering a view (`cluster.ex:106-121`), and that is what the
assertions check.

## 1. Two nodes, delayed gossip

| Step | Action | local view | database |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | this node tracks 10 | 10 | - |
| 2 | n2's 7 are gossiped but held | 10 | - |
| 3 | this node flushes | 10 | 10 |
| 4 | the held gossip is delivered | 17 | 10 |
| 5 | n2 flushes its 7 | 17 | 17 |
| 6 | n2 announces the total 17 | 17 | 17 |

Final: view 17, database 17, and step 6's announcement is asserted not to have
lowered the view.

## 2. Two nodes, lost gossip

| Step | Action | local view | database |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | this node tracks 10 | 10 | - |
| 2 | this node flushes | 10 | 10 |
| 3 | n2's delta gossip is dropped, never delivered | 10 | 10 |
| 4 | n2 flushes its 7 | 10 | 17 |
| 5 | n2 announces the total 17 | **17** | 17 |

The view is 10 before the announcement and 17 after it. Convergence comes from
the flush, not from the gossip, which is exactly the documented guarantee:
gossip narrows the window and the flush closes it.

## 3. Four nodes, mixed delayed and lost gossip

Deltas: this node 11, n2 3, n3 5, n4 9. Sum 28.
n2's gossip is delivered at once, n3's is delayed, n4's is lost outright.

| Step | Action | local view | database |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | this node tracks 11 | 11 | - |
| 2 | n2 gossips +3 | 14 | - |
| 3 | this node flushes | **11** | 11 |
| 4 | n2 flushes 3, announces 14 | 14 | 14 |
| 5 | n3's delayed gossip +5 arrives | 19 | 14 |
| 6 | n3 flushes 5, announces 19 | 19 | 19 |
| 7 | n4 flushes 9, announces 28 | 28 | 28 |
| 8 | n2 re-announces its stale 14 | **28** | 28 |

Step 3 is the documented downward move: this node re-bases on the database total
of 11 and n2's unflushed 3 leaves the view until step 4. Step 8 is Z3: an
announcement below this node's base is ignored (`cluster.ex:112`), so a late
message cannot erase a fresher base. Every announcement at steps 4, 6, 7 and 8
is asserted not to have lowered the view.

Final: database 28 = 11 + 3 + 5 + 9, local view 28.

## 4. The measured overshoot

`I05 overshoot is bounded by what other nodes admitted between announcements`.
Hard limit **50** on `:ai_generations` (the `:free` plan).

The interleaving that produced the number:

```
t0  n2 admits 20 against its own view. Its gossip is in flight and this
    node's view does not include them.
t1  this node attempts 60 reservations against its own view
      admitted here             = 50
      the 51st                  = {:error, :limit_exceeded}
t2  n2's gossip lands
      AuroraMeter.usage         = 70
t3  this node flushes its 50; n2 flushes its 20 and announces 70
      database                  = 70
      local view                = 70
```

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| limit | 50 |
| admitted on this node | 50 |
| admitted by the peer since the last announcement this node applied | 20 |
| **measured overshoot** | **20** |
| bound (what the other nodes admitted in that window) | 20 |
| durable total after both nodes flushed | 70 |

The assertion is `overshoot <= admitted_by_peer`, and the overshoot is equal to
it here because one peer admitted its whole allowance inside one window. The
overshoot is not a reporting artefact: after both nodes flush, the database
holds 70 against a limit of 50.

This number belongs in `docs/correctness.md` under I05's known limits (it is
there) and is the figure 08c re-measures under load with real PubSub timing.
