# I06: durable accepted events survive process loss without duplication

**Owner:** build unit 03b. **Contributors:** 03a (the schema and the unique
index), 03d (replay). **Gate:** G03.

## What is guaranteed, as of 03b

An event accepted by `AuroraMeter.record/4` or `record_batch/2` is written in
**one** transaction with its projection delta and the configured export intent.
The three commit together or none of them does. A caller that dies, times out or
never learns the outcome retries with the **same** `id` and gets the persisted
fact back with `:duplicate`: no second row, no second totals delta, no second
export intent.

The in-memory value `AuroraMeter.usage/2` reads is a **view** of that fact and
is applied after the commit. It can lag; the durable total from
`AuroraMeter.Events.total/3` cannot.

## Prerequisites

Core schema versions 7 and 8 (the `(tenant_key, event_id)` unique index, and
`event_id`, `payload_hash` and `occurred_at` `NOT NULL`), a storage adapter
declaring `:durable_events`, and a caller that retries with the identity it
first used. A caller that generates a fresh id per attempt gets no protection,
and cannot: identity is the caller's to supply.

## Known limits

- **The view lags a lost reply.** A caller killed after the commit and before
  the reply leaves the row, the delta and the intent correct, and the in-memory
  value low, until its retry or the next cold seed. Measured and asserted
  rather than papered over: see `03b-kill-matrix.md`.
- **A host transaction makes durability conditional.** Inside one, the work
  runs on a savepoint and the event comes back `durability: :conditional`. The
  host's rollback removes everything, which is the guarantee; the host's
  forgetting to call `AuroraMeter.Events.after_commit/1` costs only the view.
- **Nothing here is about delivery.** I06 ends at the commit. What happens to
  the export intent afterwards is I15 and belongs to Pro.
- **Replay is not proven here.** "Restart and replay reproduce the same totals"
  is 03d's, and its bullet in `docs/correctness.md` is still PLANNED.

## Measured

| Situation | Connections | Rows | Totals delta | Outbox items | Source |

| One identity, twelve simultaneous submissions | 12 independent, non-sandbox | 1 | 1 (quantity 5, events 1) | 1 | `03b-concurrency.json` |
| 192 distinct ids in one tenant and period | 12 independent, non-sandbox | 192 | 1 row: quantity 576, events 192 | n/a | `03b-concurrency.json` |
| Kill before commit | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | `03b-kill-matrix.md` |
| Kill after commit, before the reply | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | `03b-kill-matrix.md` |
| Same-id retry after that kill | 1 | still 1 | still 1 | still 1 | `03b-kill-matrix.md` |
| Host transaction rolled back | 1, non-sandbox | 0 | 0 | 0 | `record_projection_test.exs` |

Outcomes of the twelve-way race, seed 0: **1 inserted, 11 duplicate, 0
`conflict_unresolved`**.

## Tests

Indexed in `docs/correctness.md` under I06. In short:

- `AuroraMeter.RecordTest`: the duplicate contract and the per-tenant identity.
- `AuroraMeter.RecordBatchTest`: a storage failure mid-batch rolls back every new row.
- `AuroraMeter.RecordConcurrencyTest`: the twelve-connection races, the two kills, and the generation interlock.
- `AuroraMeter.RecordProjectionTest`: the host transaction, both directions.
- `AuroraMeter.MigrationV8Test` (03a): the database's own refusal of a second row under one identity.

## Negative controls

`03b-controls.json`. C3 makes the totals delta apply to duplicates as well as
inserts and the named duplicate test fails; C2 removes the payload-hash
comparison and the conflict tests fail. Each mutation was restored and the
restore verified by hash.
