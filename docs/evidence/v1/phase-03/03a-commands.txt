Two behaviours measured rather than assumed
-------------------------------------------
[2026-09-14T16:58:23Z] MIX_ENV=test mix run --no-start tmp/v1/03a/probe.exs
  exit 0. Two questions, two answers:

  Q1  Is pg_column_size(metadata) the same measure to an INSERT and to a SELECT?
      NO, and the difference is three orders of magnitude for one value.
        pg_column_size(stored jsonb)      259
        octet_length(metadata::text)      20012
        pg_column_size(metadata::text)    20016
        INSERT of that value with CHECK (pg_column_size(metadata) <= 16384):
          {:rejected, "probe_size"}
        VALIDATE CONSTRAINT over the identical stored row:
          :validated
      A constraint that refuses a value on the way in and proves it on the way
      out is not a constraint. The implementation uses
      octet_length(metadata::text) <= 16384. Map amendment 1 in 03a-report.md.

  Q2  Does a session advisory lock survive the death of the BEAM process that
      checked the connection out of the repo pool?
        advisory locks held before the kill: 1
        runner died: :killed
        after 0ms:   try_lock=false
        (DBConnection logged: disconnected, client exited)
        after 50ms:  try_lock=true
      The lock is released when the pool closes the dead client's connection,
      which took under 50 ms here. Recorded in the Backfill moduledoc and waited
      for, not slept through, in the resume test.
